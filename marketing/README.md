# Chameleon – Chapter Director Outreach (internal)

Goal: recruit student chapter directors (grades 9–11) across the US who care about STEM, sustainability and nonprofit work.

## Ground rules (read first)
This folder holds strategy and templates only. **No lead lists or personal data of individuals under 18 belong in this repo** (see `.gitignore`). Why:
- Scraping profiles and cold-messaging minors creates child-safety, privacy (COPPA/state law) and reputation risk, and violates LinkedIn/Instagram ToS.
- LinkedIn requires members to be 16+, so most 9th–10th graders are not there.
- Students who opt in or are referred by a trusted adult convert better than cold DMs.

Rules:
1. Reach students through **opt-in or trusted-adult channels** (below), not scraped profile lists.
2. Cold outreach goes to **adults/organizations** (teachers, club advisors, program staff). Students are contacted only after they've applied, replied, or been referred.
3. Any DM to a student under 18 is 1:1, public-professional in tone, copies/offers a parent or advisor, and is never on private/personal platforms (no Snapchat, texting).
4. Under-18 directors need parent/guardian consent and a second adult on the chapter's comms.
5. Keep leads in a private CRM/Sheet with access limited to the team; delete on request.

## Channels (ranked)
See `channels.md`. Short version: (1) inbound application page + social, (2) teacher/advisor partnerships, (3) competition & program alumni networks, (4) student-org newsletters, (5) public-event outreach, (6) LinkedIn for 16+ via inbound/content, not scraping.

## Files
- `channels.md` – where to find qualified students ethically, with specific orgs/programs to approach
- `templates.md` – emails/DMs for advisors, orgs, and (opt-in) students; follow-ups
- `criteria.md` – director scoring rubric used on *applicants*
- `tracker-template.md` – columns for your private CRM (organizations, adults, applicants)
- `30-day-plan.md` – weekly plan and KPIs
