# Templates

## A. Teacher / club advisor (cold email – primary)
Subject: Free student-led STEM & sustainability chapter for [School]

Hi [Name],
I'm [Your name] with Chameleon, a student-led nonprofit running STEM and sustainability chapters led by high schoolers. We're looking for a motivated 9th–11th grader at [School] to start a chapter, with support and guidelines for advisors and parents.
If a student comes to mind, could you pass along this link? [URL] Happy to send a 1-page overview or join a club meeting for 10 minutes.
Thanks for all you do,
[Name, title, contact]

## B. Program / organization partnership
Subject: Partnering to bring [Program] students into leadership roles

Hi [Name],
Chameleon helps students launch local chapters on STEM, sustainability and service. Many [Program] students are looking for the next step. Would you share a short call for chapter directors with your community (newsletter, alumni list, Slack)? We can supply copy, graphics and a co-branded page. 15 minutes this week?

## C. Opt-in student reply (only after they apply, reply, or are referred)
Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in leading a Chameleon chapter! Next steps: 1) a quick 20-min intro call (a parent or advisor is welcome to join), 2) a short plan for your chapter, 3) parent/guardian consent form. Questions? Reply here.

## D. Follow-ups
- Day 5: "Following up in case this got buried—happy to send a one-pager."
- Day 12: "Last note: here's a 90-sec video of what directors do. Glad to share with any student you recommend."
- Stop after 3 touches; honor opt-outs immediately.

## E. Public social CTA
"Want to start a STEM + sustainability chapter at your school? Chameleon is recruiting student directors in all 50 states. Apply: [URL]. Parents/teachers: reach us at [email]."
