# Director scoring rubric (applied to applicants/referrals)
Score 0–3 each; 12+ = fast-track interview.
1. Initiative: started or led a club, project, fundraiser, or event.
2. Subject fit: STEM and/or sustainability and/or nonprofit involvement.
3. Reliability: references from a teacher/advisor, consistent activity, grade 9–11 (2+ years left at school).
4. Reach: school/community network, social/organizing ability, state coverage we lack.
Plus: parent/guardian consent on file (required), advisor/sponsor identified (required).
Prioritize states/metros with no chapter yet.
