# Channels

## 1. Inbound (highest quality)
- "Start a Chameleon chapter" application page on the site (name, grade, state, school, parent/advisor email, why, past projects). Add a Google Form if the site form is heavy.
- Short-form content (Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn) from existing directors: "How I started a chapter." Use students' own public posts only with their and a guardian's permission.
- Hashtags to *publish* under (not to scrape): #highschoolstem #youthclimate #studentnonprofit #sustainability #STEMed.

## 2. Adults who already reach the students (cold outreach OK)
- High school science, environmental, robotics, and Key Club/NHS/Interact advisors; CTE and STEM coordinators; school librarians and counselors.
- District sustainability coordinators and green-schools programs.
- Public-facing directories: school websites list club advisors by name/email.

## 3. Program and competition networks (partner, ask them to forward)
Approach program staff to share a call for chapter directors with their students/alumni:
- Environmental/youth: Youth Climate / sustainability fellowships, Green Schools programs, Eco-Schools USA, Sierra Club & Audubon youth chapters, 4-H, Scouts (Eagle/Gold projects), Earth Day orgs.
- STEM: Science Olympiad, FIRST Robotics/FLL, Science Fair & regional fairs, Regeneron/ISEF-affiliated fairs, MathCounts, STEM Ambassadors programs, Girls Who Code, AI4ALL, Technovation, Civic/Congressional App Challenge.
- Nonprofit/service: Youth Philanthropy Councils, Prudential Spirit of Community, Presidential Volunteer Service Award, DoSomething, JA (Junior Achievement), Rotary Interact, local community foundations' youth boards.
- Verify each program's current contact/partnership page before reaching out.

## 4. Student-run orgs
- Peer youth nonprofits (e.g., student-founded orgs) – propose cross-promotion and director referrals between leaders, via their public contact/partnership emails.
- Teen newsletters and student media that accept submissions.

## 5. Events
- Regional science fairs, youth sustainability summits, Earth Day events, virtual hackathons – table, sponsor a prize, or ask to give a 5-min "start a chapter" talk.

## 6. LinkedIn (16+ only, compliant)
- Post chapter wins from the Chameleon company page; invite students to apply via link.
- Use LinkedIn Groups/Events for youth STEM where *they* join and engage; reply to students who engage with your posts.
- Don't mass-scrape, use automation, or send cold DMs to minors. Reach adult connectors (teachers, program managers) instead.

## 7. Referral loop
- Every current director gets a referral link/code; reward = recognition, certificate, letter of recommendation (not cash for minors).
